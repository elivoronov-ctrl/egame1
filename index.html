🎀 Bubble Bounce 🎀

Help Bubbles the bouncy ball hop home to Rainbow Castle!

  • Press Space (or tap / click) to jump. Hold it longer to jump higher!
  • Press Space again in the air for a double jump.
  • Dodge cupcakes, candy spikes, gift boxes, buzzy bees, grumpy clouds and ghosts.
  • You get 3 hearts each level. Bump into something and you lose one.
  • Grab ⭐ stars for points. Reach the 🚩 finish flag to clear the level.
  • 10 levels, each one faster with new obstacles. Press P to pause.
